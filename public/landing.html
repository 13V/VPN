A LITTLE MORE CONTEXT

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Still getting to know the idea?
Start here.

Is this a live VPN service?+

Not yet. The portal is a working demo of the holder experience. It can create and extend sample tunnels, but those tunnels do not connect to the internet. Live supplier provisioning and connection testing are still outstanding.

Do I need tokens or a wallet to try it?+

No. Choose “Explore the demo” to enter the portal, then start a sample workspace. You can try creating and extending a tunnel with sample credit. Wallet sign-in is optional and does not authorize a payment.

How would my allowance be funded?+

The proposed model uses token trading fees to fund a weekly service pool. Eligible holders would get an allowance from that funded pool. The token launch and exact allocation policy have not been finalized, and future allowances are not guaranteed.

What will I need to connect later?+

The planned setup uses the WireGuard app and a configuration provided after provisioning. Today’s download is a clearly labelled text example, not a working configuration. Actual connection quality, DNS behaviour and throughput still need testing.

What can you promise about privacy?+

We are not making an independently verified no-logs or anonymity claim. Supplier privacy statements and redistribution permission still need validation. We’ll publish evidence as the service is tested.
