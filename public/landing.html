A little more clarity

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can I use Velora as a VPN today?

Not yet. You can create and extend demo plans and download sample setup files. These files do not establish a real VPN connection. The interactive connection above is a simulation.

Do I need a wallet or tokens?

No wallet or tokens are needed to explore. Start a demo workspace with sample access. Wallet sign-in is optional and does not authorize a payment.

How would community funding work?

The plan is to use token trading fees to fund a shared VPN service budget. Eligible holders would receive VPN data without a separate VPN payment. Eligibility, data amounts and token launch details are not finalized; future access is not guaranteed.

What would I need to connect?

The planned service uses WireGuard and a configuration supplied with your plan. The phone shown here is a future app concept. Today’s sample text files cannot be used for a real connection.

What can you promise about privacy?

We are not making an independently verified no-logs or anonymity claim. Supplier privacy statements, redistribution permission and connection quality still require validation.
