THE CONNECTION GUIDE

A little value.
A useful connection.

This MVP lets you try the holder experience before any real access is offered.

  1. 01

    Explore your allowance

    Demo mode gives you sample funds to test creating and extending tunnels. These funds have no monetary value.

  2. 02

    Create your sample tunnel

    Choose Australia and the one-day plan. The download is a text example; it cannot connect you to a VPN.

  3. 03

    Know what comes next

    Live access requires verified supplier recovery and a payment adapter that enforces the spending cap. Holder eligibility, fee funding and the token launch are not connected yet.

Installing a real tunnel later

A live service would provide a WireGuard configuration to import into the WireGuard app. Do not import the demo text file. Tunnel connectivity, DNS and throughput are still untested.
