THE CONNECTION GUIDE

Try the demo
in three steps.

Explore sample access. Nothing here charges your wallet or connects a VPN.

  1. 01

    Start with seven sample days

    These days let you try creating and extending plans. They are only a demo limit, not an announced holder benefit.

  2. 02

    Create a one-day sample plan

    Choose Australia and the one-day plan. The download is a text example; it cannot connect you to a VPN.

  3. 03

    Preview or extend your plan

    View a sample setup file or add another sample day. Live VPN data and token-funded holder access are still being developed.

Installing a real tunnel later

A live service would provide a WireGuard configuration to import into the WireGuard app. Do not import the demo text file. Tunnel connectivity, DNS and throughput are still untested.
